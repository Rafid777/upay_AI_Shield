Responsible AI & Governance Framework Compliance Policy Active

1. Privacy & Synthetic Data

Operates exclusively on benchmark synthetic data. No real-world customer PII or production financial credentials are ever accessed or processed.

2. Explainability by Design

Every prediction is mathematically explained via SHAP TreeExplainer. Black-box opaque decisions are strictly prohibited.

3. Human-in-the-Loop Oversight

Autonomous freezing, blocking, or asset denial is strictly disabled. The system acts solely as decision-support; consequential decisions remain with human analysts.

4. Role Division Principle

XGBoost: Mathematical risk scoring.
SHAP: Feature attribution explainability.
Gemini: Narrative synthesis & inquiry assistant.
Human Analyst: Final consequential decision.

5. Security & Secrets Management

All credentials and API keys reside in server-side environment variables (.env). The client bundle never exposes keys or sensitive endpoints.

6. Limitations & Scope

This software is a hackathon innovation prototype. Pre-production testing and localized regulatory audits are required before production banking deployment.
